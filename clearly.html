A little space for yourself.

Clear.ly brings simple reflection workbooks and a personal diary to your iPhone and iPad.

Write. Reflect. Return.

Choose an everyday topic: money, stress, anxious thoughts, feeling stuck or memories. Each workbook follows four steps: describe where you are, consider how you would like to respond, reflect, and save.

Keep drafts, return to your notes and compare your optional before-and-after ratings. There is no target score.

Your writing stays with you

The workbooks and diary work offline. Your journal and questionnaire answers stay on your device. No account, purchases or subscription are required in version 1.0.0.

Clear.ly is a journaling tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

Support

For help with Clear.ly, email direct@eponymicalgames.com. Include your app version, device model and a description of the problem. Please do not send private diary entries.

Common questions

How do I resume a draft?

Open My diary, choose Drafts, open an entry and tap Continue this workbook.

How do I delete an entry?

Open the entry, scroll to Delete entry, and confirm. Deletion is permanent.

Can I move my diary to another device?

There is no cloud sync or automatic diary backup. Keep a separate copy of anything you want to retain before deleting the app or changing devices.

How can I change my privacy choices?

Open Settings → Allow usage analytics. You can switch analytics on or off at any time; your diary and workbooks remain available.
