Delete your Learn Piano account

Learn Piano: Play Songs is provided by Eponymical Games Ltd. You can delete your account in the Android app or request deletion by email.

In the Android app

  1. Open the navigation menu and choose Profile.
  2. Scroll to your account and select Delete account.
  3. Confirm the request. If the app asks for a recent sign-in, sign out, sign in again and repeat the deletion.

If deletion fails, contact us using the steps below.

Without the app

  1. Email direct@eponymicalgames.com with the subject Delete my Learn Piano account.
  2. Tell us the email address and sign-in provider you used for Learn Piano. If you used Sign in with Apple and Hide My Email, include the Apple relay address.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before completing the request. Never send your password.

What is deleted and retained

Successful in-app deletion removes your Firebase sign-in account and the cloud profile containing synced course progress, achievements and related progress records. These records are removed when deletion completes; they are not retained as an active account.

Local songs, recordings, practice history and settings may remain on your device. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove those local copies. An email request does not remotely erase files on your devices.

Purchase and entitlement records are separate from the sign-in account. Google Play, Apple and purchase service providers may retain transaction records under their own retention policies. Records needed for legal, accounting, fraud prevention or dispute handling are retained for the applicable required period. If we retain data in response to your request, we will explain the categories, reason and applicable retention period.

You may also email us to request deletion of specific data while keeping your account.

Cancel subscriptions separately

Deleting an account does not cancel a subscription. On Android, open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. For an Apple subscription, use your Apple subscription settings.
